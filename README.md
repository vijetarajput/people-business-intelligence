# People & Business Intelligence Platform

**Building a B2B intelligence platform for professional research, business relationships, and informed decision-making.**

## Overview

The People & Business Intelligence Platform is an early-stage SaaS project exploring how businesses can research high-profile professionals, understand their publicly documented business interests, and prepare for important business interactions more efficiently.

The platform aims to bring fragmented information from reliable public sources into structured, searchable profiles with source-backed insights.

It is designed for business professionals and organisations—not for general celebrity browsing.

## Problem Statement

Researching a business leader, founder, or investor can require reviewing numerous websites, company announcements, interviews, news articles, and public records.

This information is often fragmented, difficult to verify, and time-consuming to organise.

The project explores how a dedicated intelligence platform could make this research more efficient, structured, and useful for professional decision-making.

## Vision

To build a professional intelligence platform that connects people, companies, publicly disclosed investments, and documented business relationships in one searchable workspace.

## Proposed Features

- **People Search:** Find professionals using names, organisations, and relevant business information.
- **Professional Profiles:** Organise career history, company affiliations, and publicly documented activities.
- **Business Intelligence:** Research companies, investment disclosures, and relevant business developments.
- **Relationship Mapping:** Explore documented connections between people and organisations.
- **AI Research Assistant:** Generate answers grounded in accessible sources.
- **Meeting Briefs:** Prepare concise, source-linked research briefs for professional meetings.
- **Research Updates:** Explore ways to monitor relevant public announcements and changes.

These are proposed capabilities and may change as customer research and product development progress.

## Target Users

Potential customer segments include:

- Luxury and private aviation brokers
- Family offices and investment professionals
- Luxury real estate advisers
- Strategic partnerships and business development teams
- Professional advisers working with business leaders

The initial customer segment will be selected through customer interviews and market validation.

## Product Principles

1. **Evidence first:** Important claims should link to their sources.
2. **Accuracy over speculation:** Distinguish verified facts, estimates, and unconfirmed reports.
3. **Transparency:** Show source dates and acknowledge information gaps.
4. **Responsible research:** Use appropriate sources and respect privacy, data rights, and applicable laws.
5. **Business-focused design:** Prioritise practical research workflows over general-purpose browsing.

## Development Status

**Stage:** Product discovery and MVP planning.

The project is being developed iteratively, beginning with customer problem validation, product requirements, data modelling, and technical architecture.

The repository will document product decisions, specifications, implementation progress, and lessons learned as development proceeds.

## Planned Technology Stack

The initial technology choices under consideration include:

- **Frontend:** Next.js
- **Backend and database:** Supabase and PostgreSQL
- **AI capabilities:** Large language models with source-grounded retrieval
- **Search:** Structured data and full-text search
- **Development environment:** Cursor and GitHub

The final architecture will depend on validated requirements, data-source availability, and technical testing.
